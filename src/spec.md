# Specification

## Summary
**Goal:** Build a KAYSEN Botanical Luxury product showcase website with stop-motion aesthetic, featuring theatrical diorama staging, mechanical animations, and scroll-triggered sequences.

**Planned changes:**
- Create design specification documenting stop-motion aesthetic, color palette (deep emerald, botanical greens, luxury gold), typography, and mechanical animation timing principles
- Build Hero section with diorama-style stage layout establishing KAYSEN Botanical Luxury brand
- Build The Unveiling sequence with articulated mechanical hands revealing the KAYSEN bottle through ratcheted movements
- Build The Focus section with mechanical spotlight using ratchet joint animation to highlight product
- Build The Wave section with spring mechanism physics creating flowing botanical water effects
- Implement audio design with plastic mechanism sounds synchronized to animations
- Build Product Details section showcasing specifications, ingredients, and botanical story
- Build Editions showcase displaying different KAYSEN product variants
- Polish all animations for consistent stop-motion timing and smooth section transitions

**User-visible outcome:** Users experience a theatrical, single-page scroll website showcasing KAYSEN Botanical Luxury products through mechanical stop-motion animations, with articulated hands unveiling the bottle, spotlight focusing on details, flowing water features, and synchronized tactile audio feedback throughout the journey.
